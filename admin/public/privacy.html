DukaSmart Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

DukaSmart is an app that helps shops and small businesses in Kenya record sales, expenses, deni (customer debts), stock, branches and staff. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the DukaSmart app, why we need it, who we share it with, and the choices you have, including how to delete your account.

We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. If anything here is unclear, write to us at morricemwendwa4@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Business name, type of business and location
  • Owner's name and phone number
  • Your PIN. We only keep a scrambled (hashed) version, so nobody at DukaSmart can read it.
  • An invite code, if someone referred you

When you use the app

  • Sales, expenses, products and stock movements you record
  • Customers you add for deni, including their names, phone numbers and balances
  • SMS reminders you send: the number, the message and when it was sent
  • Branches you create, and staff you add (their names, phone numbers, roles and a hashed PIN)
  • Which staff member recorded each entry, and when

When you pay for a subscription

  • The M-Pesa phone number used, the amount, the plan chosen, and the M-Pesa receipt number and status that Safaricom sends back. We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your GPS location, contacts list, photos, messages or advertising IDs, and the app has no ads.

How we use it

  • To run the app: saving your records, backing them up and syncing them across your phones and staff
  • To sign you and your staff in, and control what each staff member can see and do
  • To process subscription payments and manage your free trial and plan
  • To give referral rewards to whoever invited you
  • To give support, for example resetting a forgotten PIN after confirming it is you
  • To contact you by phone or SMS about your account, payments or important changes

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your business records for advertising.

Your customers' details

When you record a customer for deni, you decide what to enter about them. For that information you are responsible for having a fair reason to keep it and for telling your customers if they ask. We store it so you can track what they owe.

If you or your manager choose to send an SMS reminder, we send that message to the customer's phone number on your behalf, using the wording you approve. We keep a record of each reminder (the number, the message and when it was sent) so you can see your history and so we can count your SMS. We never send your customers anything you did not ask us to send, and we never use their numbers for our own marketing.

Who we share it with

We only share information with companies that help us run DukaSmart, and only what they need:

  • Safaricom (M-Pesa): your phone number and the amount, so the payment request can reach your phone
  • Africa's Talking: the customer's phone number and the reminder text, only when you send an SMS reminder
  • Render: runs our server
  • Neon: hosts our database
  • Netlify: hosts our website and admin panel

We may also share information if the law requires it, for example a valid court order.

Where it is stored

Your records are saved on your phone first, so the app works without internet. When you are online they are copied to our database, which is hosted by Neon in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), with our server run by Render. This means your data is transferred outside Kenya. We use these providers because they protect data to a standard at least as strong as Kenyan law requires.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your records so you can see your history.
  • If your subscription ends, we keep your data so you can pick up where you left off when you renew, unless you ask us to delete it.
  • When you delete your account, your business records are removed from our database straight away. Database backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • We may keep payment records longer where tax or accounting law requires it.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • PINs are stored only in hashed form.
  • Your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage.
  • Staff only see the branch and records their role allows.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires. Keep your PIN private and remove staff who leave.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Be told how your data is used (this page)
  • Get a copy of your data. Owners and managers can export sales, expenses, deni and stock to a spreadsheet (CSV) from the Account tab, or you can email us and we will send it.
  • Correct wrong information. You can edit most records in the app yourself.
  • Object to some uses of your data
  • Have your data deleted (see below)
  • Complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke

DukaSmart is meant for business owners and their staff, not children under 18.

Delete your DukaSmart account

Deleting your account permanently removes your business and everything recorded under it: sales, expenses, deni, customers, products and stock, branches, staff accounts and payment history. This cannot be undone. If you might need your records later, use Export records in the Account tab before you delete.

Option 1: In the app (owners)

  1. Open DukaSmart and sign in as the owner.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  4. Enter your PIN to confirm.

Option 2: Ask us

If you can't open the app, send an email to morricemwendwa4@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my DukaSmart account". Include your business name and the phone number you registered with. We will call that number to confirm it is you, then delete the account within 7 days and reply to let you know.

Staff members

Staff accounts belong to the business. Ask the owner to remove you from the Staff screen, or email us and we will arrange it with the owner.

Referral rewards already paid to the person who invited you are not reversed. Leads records we created before you signed up may be kept for our own sales follow-up unless you ask us to remove them too.
